A colorful one-tap arcade game by Alen Brunec / AB DIGITECH.

How to play

A winged basketball flies down the street. Tap anywhere to flap and rise; let go to fall. Drop the ball through each hoop from above to score and knock over the street lamp behind it. Miss the hoop and you crash into the lamp. Collect coins on the way; bonus hoops below the main hoop are worth two points. Day and night city streets alternate as you go. Your best score, last score and coin total are stored on your device. Use the speaker button to toggle sound.

Need help?

Contact support@abdigitech.net. Please include your device model, iOS or Android version and a short description of the issue. Do not send passwords or payment details.

No account is required to play. The game shows Google AdMob advertising between attempts and privacy choices where required. See the privacy policy for advertising data and consent details.
